Dees Pecan Company

Farm in Grand Bay, Mobile County

Dees Pecan Company opened in 1925, and has remained a family owned business. We sell pecans, coffee, and confections. Our hours are Monday to Saturday from 9 to 6 during fall because it is our busiest season!
