Blue Basket Farms

Farm in Milton, Santa Rosa County, FL

Popular family-run u-pick farm north of Milton, on the former Lundy Blueberry land, specializing in blueberries and sunflowers with some u-pick squash, zucchini and green beans, plus local honey. Southern Highbush and Rabbiteye varieties.
