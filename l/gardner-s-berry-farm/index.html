Gardner's Berry Farm

Farm in Robertsdale, Baldwin County

Small family-owned and operated U pick and We pick Blackberry and Blueberry farm. Sales are based on gallon lots, open seven days a week 7:00 AM till dusk as the growing season allows late April through early July. Appointments are not required unless products are overpicked and new product requires time to ripen. Check the web page for when the season opens and notice for appointment requirements. Church groups are welcome. Payments by cash, personal check (local) and Credit/Debit Card.
