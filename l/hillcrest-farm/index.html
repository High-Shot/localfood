Hillcrest Farm

Farm in Elberta, Baldwin County

U-pick blueberries and muscadines with a year-round farm store: brown eggs, honey from hives on the farm, preserves, hot sauce, ice cream, and home-grown produce. Four miles west of the Lillian bridge on Highway 98.
