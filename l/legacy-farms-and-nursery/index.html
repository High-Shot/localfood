Legacy Farms and Nursery

Farm in Citronelle, Mobile County

At Legacy Farms we are continuing a way of life that started for our families in the early 1900's. Though our products have changed through the years our work ethic and commitment to the farm has not. Today on the farm you will find ferns and ornamentals hanging in our greenhouses. When you get to our pastures, we are raising beef cattle and hay. The bulk of the production area is covered with several varieties of pecans that were started in 2012. Actively working daily on the farm are three generations caring for the land God has made us stewards over! We market ferns and colorful hanging baskets in the spring starting in February. In the summer we are gathering and selling hay. When October rolls around our attention turns to the pecans and a few Garden mums that we grow.
