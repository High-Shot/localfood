Bayou Pecan Co LLC

Farm in Irvington, Mobile County

Bayou Pecan Co LLC also known to friends and family as Landry Farms. Our retail store is now open, we would like for you to come visit us, we have Pecans, Honey, Satsumas, Pecan oil and specilty gifts for everone. We produce Pecans and Hay, the orchards total 65 acres, 1200 trees and 17 varieties of pecans. Hay is produced between the tree rows during the summer months.
