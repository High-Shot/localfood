Bartl Farms

Farm in Robertsdale, Baldwin County

Real farmers, real meat! Our mission is to help our customers gain access to high quality beef that they can trust! Through best farming practices, we strive to build up our land resources that support our cattle and crops for generations to come. Established in 1914, Bartl Farms is a fourth-generation family farm located in Central Baldwin County, Alabama. We consider raising quality cattle to produce quality beef for your family a high privilege that we do not take for granted. The beef you purchase from us is from cattle who are raised on our farm. They are grass-fed and grain finished. Their feed is made on our farm with only the best ingredients grown on our farm. Their food never consists of hormones or antibiotics.
