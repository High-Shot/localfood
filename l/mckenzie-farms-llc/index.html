McKenzie Farms LLC

FARM Farm in Fairhope, Baldwin County

We are a fourth generation family farm! Our father, Billy, raised all four of his children on this farm and worked every day of his life until his passing in 2013. Oldest brothers Tommy and Mike McKenzie currently run the farm, while other family members help out. We grow a large variety of produce, but red potatoes and cabbage are our two main crops.
