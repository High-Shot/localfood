Price Pecan Farm

FARM Farm in Lillian, Baldwin County

Price Pecan Farm is a small, family owned and operated farm that’s been in my family since I helped my father plant the first pecan trees in the mid 1970’s. We are located in the Southeastern corner of Baldwin County, a few miles from Perdido Bay. We sell whole pecans, Shelled Pecan halves, and Shelled Baker’s Pieces directly from our farm. We sell our product year round, Mondays through Saturdays. Hours of operation vary by season. Customers can reach us anytime by phone, text, email, or Facebook messenger.
