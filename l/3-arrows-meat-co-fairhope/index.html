Farm store in Fairhope, Baldwin County

3 Arrows Meat Co. Spanish Fort & Fairhope, Alabama Founded in Spanish Fort, Alabama, 3 Arrows Meat Co. is a family-owned boutique butcher shop serving Baldwin County with locations in both Spanish Fort and Fairhope. The original Spanish Fort store was established with a simple mission: bring premium meats, local products, and genuine hospitality back to the neighborhood butcher counter. Named after the Hilbun family’s three children, 3 Arrows is rooted in faith, family, and community. Our goal is to create a welcoming environment where customers feel like family and leave confident in the quality of the food they’re serving at their own tables. Premium Meats & Gulf Coast Seafood At 3 Arrows, quality is the standard.
