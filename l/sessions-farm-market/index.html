Sessions Farm Market

Farm in Grand Bay, Mobile County

Our farm is a 3rd generation operation. We are retail and wholesale and grow peaches, plums, tomatoes, sweet corn, squash, cantaloupes, watermelons, green beans, cabbage, turnips, collards, and pecans. Our road side market is open year around.
