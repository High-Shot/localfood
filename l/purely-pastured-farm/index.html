Purely Pastured Farm

Farm in Foley, Baldwin County

Robert and Maegen Benford operate a 20-acre farm in Foley, AL. The farm currently raises pastured chicken and pastured pork. We are adding a pastured egg enterprise in January 2022 and hope to add beef as well. We utilize regenerative farming practices to minimize erosion and promote healthy soil.
