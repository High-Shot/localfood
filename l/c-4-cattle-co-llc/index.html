C-4 Cattle Co., LLC

Farm in Grand Bay, Mobile County

C-4 Cattle Co., LLC is a Veteran-Owned, family operated ranch in Southern Alabama that raises cattle, lamb, and pigs. We believe in a natural and sustainable approach to ranching. Our animals are raised on open pastures, and are only given the highest quality of feed and supplements. We take great care in every step of the process, from breeding to your freezer. We strive to provide affordable products to fit any budget, therefore, we do not have an open storefront. To place an order, please reach out via phone, email, or any of our socials.
