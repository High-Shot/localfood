Zirlott's Gulf Products has been in business for nearly 40 years! We are a 5th generation commercial fishing family. We catch, process and sell our seafood products from local Alabama waters. Our small family run shop has earned a reputation for selling quality, fresh local crab, shrimp, fish & oysters. Stuffed shrimp, Crab cakes, Gumbo, Crab quiche, Stuffed crab & Breaded crab claws are just a few of our most popular products.
