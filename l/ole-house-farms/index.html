Ole’ House Farms

Farm in Loxley, Baldwin County

At Ole’ House Farms we grow several varieties of potatoes, corn, squash, cucumbers, watermelon, green beans and variety of peas . We sell to local farmer market, a local grocery store as well as right out of our barn. We are a small operation and fortunately we have great friends that help us and in turn get fresh vegetables to share with their families. Tim grew potatoes for Frito Lay many years ago before he took a different career path. But he just couldn’t get the dirt out of his nails! He absolutely loves planting a seed and watch it grow. At Ole’ House Farms we pride ourselves in providing good quality vegetables to our locals. Another thing we are proud of is our relationship with Prodisee Pantry and Feeding the Gulf Coast. Social media has been a good avenue for us. Our customers contact us to see what we have and we are always available, to make arrangements for pick up. Brenda’
