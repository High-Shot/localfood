Coastal Honey Farm

Farm in Lillian, Baldwin County

Our passion is to raise healthy bees and produce the finest Honey in South Alabama. Keeping honey bees has been in the Green family since 1956 when Matt's Father, Irving "Pete" Green first started with bees. We continue today with a love for our bees and the love for pure honey.
