Local Lettuce Heads Farm

Farm in Loxley, Baldwin County, AL

Hydroponic greenhouse farm in Loxley, formerly Craine Creek Farm. Grows lettuces, salad greens and herbs year-round in controlled-environment greenhouses, without pesticides. Over thirty lettuce varieties plus seasonal greens such as watercress, mizuna, tatsoi and chard. Sells direct from the farm, at the Coastal Alabama Farmers and Fishermens Market in Foley, and wholesale to restaurants and markets from Pensacola to New Orleans.
