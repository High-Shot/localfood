Lillian Cattle Company

FARM Farm in Elberta, Baldwin County

We are a fourth generation farm located in southern Baldwin county. We grow corn, cotton, soybeans, hay, and peanuts, but our specialty is our beef! We sell whole and half steers, as well as single cuts of beef. Living so close to the Gulf, we believe the cattle are raised in paradise and you can taste the happiness and contentment in every bite! We are happy to deliver locally, even to our vacationing friends in Gulf Shores and Orange Beach! From our family farm to your family's table - give us a call!
