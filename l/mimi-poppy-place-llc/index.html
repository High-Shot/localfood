Mimi & Poppy Place LLC

FARM Farm in Semmes, Mobile County

Mimi and Poppy's Place is our family homestead, located in Semmes Alabama. We operate a small acreage homestead and offer pasture raised, grassfed lamb, pasture raised pork, and natural foraged honey from our hives located on our homestead with access to over 9,000 acres of wild, natural forage. We offer these items at farmers markets and events throughout our neighboring communities.
