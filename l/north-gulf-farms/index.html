North Gulf Farms

Seafood dock in Bayou La Batre, Mobile County, AL

At the heart of North Gulf Farms are the Gulf oyster farmers themselves—hardworking stewards of land and sea, producing high-quality shellfish without the resources to scale. We're here to change that. North Gulf Farms isn't just about oysters. We're about building a sustainable future for the South—amplifying local voices, delivering consistent products, and redefining what American oysters can be.
