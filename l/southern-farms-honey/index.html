Southern Farms Honey

Farm in Spanish Fort, Baldwin County, AL

Southern Farms Honey was established in May of 2019. We have been tending to bee hives with a fascination for years. Each year, during the honey harvest, we get requests for our delicious honey. We decided to share our love of honey with others through Southern Farms Honey. We offer raw unfiltered wildflower honey in squeeze bottles, glass jars, or decorative gift jars. Honey straws in variety of flavors add a fun addition to your coffee, tea, or simply used as a quick sweet snack. Want a little spice for your cooking? Try our Stingin' Sweet Honey, which is infused with habanero and jalapeno peppers. It tastes delicious on smoked ribs, chicken, or vegetables. You can find our honey in a few local grocery stores and gift shops or have it delivered to your door through our website.
