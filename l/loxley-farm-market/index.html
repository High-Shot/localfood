Loxley Farm Market

Farm store in Loxley, Baldwin County, AL

Family-run farm store on Highway 59, operated by the Forland family since 1995. Sells their own and neighboring farms produce plus eggs, local meats, seafood, cheeses and pantry goods, with phone and online orders and local delivery.
